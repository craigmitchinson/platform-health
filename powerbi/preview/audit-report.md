## Part 3: the app

Evidence: `npm run build`, dist confirmed git-ignored, dist/goto.html setting `page` in localStorage key platform-health-v2, `vite preview --port 5181`, headless Edge at 1920x1080 on all 21 page ids (the report page renders at 85 per cent inside the service chrome), then the browser renders of every Deneb audit block from render-blocks.ts. goto.html was deleted and the server stopped afterwards. Where the app and the Power BI manifest differ, both are noted: the manifest is the build and the app is the reference design.

Cross-page observations, which apply everywhere unless a page says otherwise:

- Kicker scheme is inconsistent. Executive pages read "PLATFORM HEALTH · OVERVIEW" (page name), domain pages read "PLATFORM HEALTH · OPERATIONS · STABILITY" (report and page), Structure and skills reads "PLATFORM HEALTH · DELIVERY AND PEOPLE" (report only) and Lineage reads "PLATFORM HEALTH · DATA AND SOURCES" (report only). Use report · page everywhere.
- Title scheme is inconsistent. Executive, Structure and Lineage pages have editorial titles ("Where the platform stands today"); domain pages carry only the domain name ("Stability"), which repeats the kicker and the navigation.
- Card titles use two styles. Mono uppercase kickers (HEAT BY LAB, BY TEAM AND METRIC, READ-OUT, SOURCES, ROLE COMPOSITION BY LAB) sit beside serif sentence-case titles drawn by Deneb specs (Priority actions, Domain score by team, Team ranking over six quarters, What moved over the period, Why the composite moved, Maturity by capability). In the app they are all mono kickers; the specs should follow.
- Band pills on domain KPI tiles show the domain's band, not the metric's. Service maturity marks "Resolution and problem management 3.3 of 5 against 4.0" and "Mean time to acknowledge 16.2 against 15" as HEALTHY although both miss target; Cost marks "Orphaned spend" WATCH although it improved by £7,600. Score the metric or drop the pill.
- Estate and Delivery use a grey CONTEXT pill on unscored tiles; no other page does. Keep it, but explain it once in the legend line.
- The header row wastes a 520x112 area under the lab legend on every page (the 132px gap in Part 1).
- The footer "Sources:" line repeats the Sources card on every domain page.
